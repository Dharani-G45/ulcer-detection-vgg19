The fine-tuned VGG19 model classifies gastrointestinal images into four categories.

Normal gastrointestinal anatomical images.

Esophagitis

Endoscopic patterns classified as esophagitis.

Ulcerative Colitis

Endoscopic patterns classified as ulcerative colitis.

Polyps
